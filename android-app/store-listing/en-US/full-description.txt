WHY RAINWATER HARVESTING?
One millimetre of rain on one square metre of roof gives one litre of water. A typical 5 marla house in Islamabad can collect about 76,000 litres a year, and in Rawalpindi about 87,000. Stored rainwater covers flushing, washing and gardening, cuts tanker bills, and the overflow can recharge groundwater through a recharge well instead of flooding the street during the monsoon.
