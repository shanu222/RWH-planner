1. A planning tool

RWH Planner gives early-stage planning estimates for rooftop rainwater harvesting and groundwater recharge in Pakistan. Results use long-term average rainfall, typical quantities and published or live market rates. Real rainfall, site conditions and prices vary.

2. Not a stamped design

The results, reports and AI answers are guidance only. Have a qualified engineer check the final design, structural loads, soil infiltration and water quality before you build. Get final quotes from local suppliers. Do not rely on AI-generated answers for safety-critical decisions.

3. Rates and sources

Rates come from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, published price lists, a maintained rate sheet or administrator updates, each shown with its source and date. We try to keep them current but do not guarantee them.
