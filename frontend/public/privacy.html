Privacy policy

RWH Planner (website and Android app “RWH Planner – Rainwater Harvesting”) · Resilience Technologies Labs · Last updated 26 November 2026

RWH Planner helps you plan a rooftop rainwater harvesting system in Pakistan. We built it to work without an account. This policy explains what data the website and the Android app handle, why, and the choices you have.

1. No accounts, designs stay on your device

You do not sign up or log in. Your planner inputs (city, roof area, household size, tank size, edited rates), saved designs, PDF reports you create, your language, units and theme are stored only on your device. We cannot see them unless you send them to our server for one of the features below.

2. Data sent to our server

FeatureWhat is sentWhyKept
Rates, videos, app settingsThe city you selectedTo return current construction rates, the video list and app settingsNot stored
PDF design reportYour current design inputs and the report languageTo generate the PDFNot stored; the PDF is returned to you
AI assistant and AI design reviewYour question, the recent chat messages and your design inputsTo generate an answerNot stored by us; answers to identical questions may be cached in server memory for up to one hour

Like every web server, ours sees your IP address. We keep it in memory only for up to 10 minutes to limit abuse (for example, too many reports from one address). We do not log the content of AI questions.

3. AI providers

To answer AI questions, our server forwards the question, the recent chat and your design figures to one of these providers: OpenAI, Google (Gemini) or OpenRouter. We do not send your name, contact details or device identifiers. Each provider processes the text under its own terms and privacy policy. AI answers are generated automatically, are labelled “AI-generated” in the app, may contain mistakes and are guidance only. Please do not type personal information into the assistant.

4. Advertising (Android app)

The Android app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions (for example, views and taps) and app usage related to ads;
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data of the ads SDK.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). The app is not directed to children, and ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Notifications

Seasonal maintenance reminders are scheduled on your phone only if you switch them on. No data is sent to us for them.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos, precise location or files.
  • We do not build a profile of you.

7. Security

The app and website talk to our server only over encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Children

RWH Planner is meant for adults planning a building’s water system. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Deleting your data

Because there is no account and we do not store your designs, you control all of your data on your device:

  • Delete a design or a report from More → My designs or More → Reports.
  • Remove cached rates, videos and thumbnails in Settings → Clear cache.
  • Remove everything by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

For data held by Google for advertising, use your Google account and Android ad settings. If you have any question about data deletion, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes and show the new date above. Contact: shahnawaz@sustainablesolution360.com.
